About Me

A tutor who adapts to you

I'm Madalena, a mathematics educator with a deep passion for investigating how people learn. I graduated with a BSc in Mathematics with First-Class Honours and went on to study Part III at the University of Cambridge, one of the most rigorous postgraduate programmes in the world.

I've been tutoring since 2021, first alongside my own studies, and now full-time. I've worked with students through Tutorberry Ltd, Parallel Academy and The King's Factor, as well as privately. Every student I work with gets a genuinely personalised experience.

My approach is grounded in maths education research, particularly pedagogy and cognitive neuroscience, so the way I teach is informed by what actually helps things stick, not just what gets you through the next test.

Above all, I'm patient and skilled at meeting students exactly where they are.
